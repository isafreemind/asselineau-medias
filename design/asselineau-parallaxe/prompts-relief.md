## Carte de profondeur complète

Entrées : affiche originale, première carte tronquée à corriger. Sortie : `profondeur-complete.png`.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image 1 is the ORIGINAL POSTER and is the geometric alignment reference. Input image 2 is the DEPTH MAP TO CORRECT. Create a corrected grayscale geometric DEPTH MAP of the ENTIRE visible man across the FULL HEIGHT of the original poster, from cropped hair at top down to the very bottom edge. The previous depth map wrongly cuts the body at the name banner, leaving the bottom third black: correct this. Keep the head and facial geometry aligned with image 1; extend anatomical neck, rounded shoulders, jacket lapels, shirt front and tie/chest all the way to the bottom, in the same positions and proportions as the original image. Imagine the poster lettering and QR code are transparent and continue the man's chest underneath them. All of the lower third must contain continuous smooth gray depth for the torso, jacket and tie, NOT a black rectangle. Remove ALL text/banner/logo/QR patterns from the depth map: do not assign them zero depth holes, instead fill with the underlying continuous body depth. This is a technical depth map, NOT a shaded photo or a clay render: use white for nearer surfaces (nose tip brightest), pale gray for curved forehead/cheeks, medium gray for ears/neck, darker medium gray for torso/rounded shoulders/jacket; use pure black ONLY for the original empty turquoise background outside the person. Skin pores, wrinkles, hair strands, pupils, clothing colors and lighting must NOT be encoded as surface color; keep depth gradients very smooth. Preserve exact original 1890:2693 aspect ratio, original silhouette and feature positions, no new framing, no perspective change, no captions, no added people. FULL BODY DEPTH TO BOTTOM EDGE, no horizontal cutoff anywhere.
